# Collective intelligence: implementation and measured evidence

## Follow-up audit: supersedes the initial causal claim

The initial implementation below had confirmed defects: an exploiting runner
could be countered by its own zero-length lane; territory/exposure were sampled
after loss or unrelated circulation; factual mailboxes could overwrite each
other; aggregate capped-memory merge differed from chronological online updates;
predicted balls were not co-located with receivers and every leg restarted the
temporal horizon. Checkpoint validation was insufficient. Consequently the
initial evidence is historical, **not complete causal proof**.

### Corrective implementation and final gates

- Complementary depth targets use opposite lateral lanes; candidate and live
  clamping reject duty collapse under validated minimum separation. Exploiting
  gives the runner factual ownership, no self-pass blockage or Link→Runner bias,
  and no automatic successful completion on timeout.
- Authoritative facts reduce directly in EventApply drain order. Expected
  receptions advance ownership; unrelated completed circulation explicitly ends
  as `Deviated`. Reward uses an owned frontier, not rival/post-deviation movement.
  Shots require coherent ownership and connection; expired commitments time out.
  No pending single-shot/pass mailbox remains.
- Accepted world results apply deduplicated terminal events chronologically to
  the actual prior, identically to online learning, rather than merging capped
  aggregate Evidence. Evidence is diagnostics only. Fractional-cap rounding is
  bounded explicitly. Six consecutive actual career fixtures test saturation,
  duplicate rejection and next-kickoff identity; real accepted match and seasonal
  SQLite roundtrips are tested in Client.
- Context is territory × observed local pressure × pattern: 24 bounded cells.
  Confidence is outcome evidence, not execution familiarity or a trained network.
- Anticipation co-locates received ball/carrier, spends one cumulative horizon,
  projects observed opponent movement and legal ball/halfway offside constraints,
  uses actual goalkeeper roles and configured pass launch/distance gain. Carry
  forecasts spend time, stay pitch-bounded and have confidence-weighted value.
  Bounded beam search evaluates competing continuations, forbids return-pass
  cycles, counts attempted candidates and fairly shares continuation work.
- Checkpoint **6**, career schema **10** reject old payloads without migration.
  Decoder validates chronology/stages/identities/tactics/finite state; restoration
  additionally checks configured caps and actual input participant identity.
- Anticipation-off seed2 exposed a pre-existing stale advantage overwriting
  halftime and exhausting the unchanged 400000 tick limit. A failing production
  regression preceded the fix: interrupted advantages now retire at stoppage.

## Contract

`TeamAttack` owns one identity-bound attacking episode per team. Four geometrical
alternatives compete: third man, attract/switch, depth and secure progression.
Link, runner and security duties are distinct, reachable outfield participants.
Intent consumes bounded continuous support below authoritative reception,
restart and defensive duties. Decision only biases a pass that its existing
feasibility filter already admitted; it never mandates a combination.

Individual continuation search projects observed motion over estimated pass
flight, bounded by horizon and ability/cognition/condition confidence. It does
not see the rival's pending decisions. Softmax uses maximum subtraction to avoid
overflow at low temperatures without changing the intended distribution.

Real completed deliveries advance milestones. Own flight and contested loose
contacts preserve a commitment; rival controlled possession, a held blocked
route or the deadline end it. Shots from involved receivers after a connection
can complete it. Restarts, departures and instruction changes clear duties
without inventing a training reward. Full-time clears commitments.

Online learning stores 12 bounded contextual estimates (three territorial
contexts times four patterns). It updates once per football termination using
realized progress/threat/space and failure cost, not an announced success.
Selection combines current feasible geometry, learned value and uncertainty.
This is an online contextual learner, not a pretrained model or a trained
neural network. Individual anticipation is a bounded predictive calculation;
it is not a separately trained individual policy.

Kickoff priors and new-match evidence are separate. Durable terminal events
carry the learning key; accepted fresh fixture outcomes merge only new samples,
including AI and user-worker paths. Season turnover retains discounted sample
mass. Observation capture is never a source of sporting learning or RNG.

Checkpoint **5** explicitly encodes and verifies episode, participant identities,
factual frontiers and both estimate maps against replay. Format 4 is rejected.
Career schema **9** persists club memory and original execution inputs; schemas
8 and 7 are rejected without migration. Removed `PlanTTL` and `ActivePlan` have
no compatibility adapters.

## Remaining limits

The learner is intentionally basic club-level contextual memory. It does not
learn a separate per-player long-horizon policy, identify opponent-specific
causal responses, or model several concurrent attacking episodes. Pattern
geometry is hand-designed. Paired evidence measures collective enabled/disabled,
not every component separately, nor convergence over repeated seasons. Those
broader research claims remain unverified despite passing implementation gates.

# Collective intelligence — working evidence

## Existing ownership

`TeamPerceive` owns the shared phase and coarse pressure/territory blackboard. `TeamDecide` translates the manager's tactics into a persistent strategic directive. `TeamPosition` produces the formation and defensive responsibilities; `TeamTransition` owns loss episodes and their reasons. `TeamAttack` owns sustained attacking patterns and duties. `PlayerIntent` arbitrates these against reception and individual commitments; steering executes the resulting intent. The scheduler and event-drain visibility are unchanged.

## Changes in this work

When a rival controls the ball and no defensive transition is active, `TeamPosition` now uses the existing defensive task machinery for organized defense. The tactical engagement line and press intensity gate pressure; available outfielders are selected by the existing fatigue-aware arrival estimate and handoff margin, while two deeper players are reserved. Coverage, screens and depth/recovery tasks share the same one-slot task array as transitions. `PlayerIntent` consumes those tasks while the rival controls the ball. Reacquisition and loose-ball handling clear them through the existing role assignment path. Tasks are already included in `DefensiveCheckpoint`.

Fallback support selection is performed once per team rather than once per player, and is suppressed while an attacking episode owns the support responsibilities. The latter does not override a committed receive run.

## Known gaps and validation limits

This is an incremental change, not the requested complete collective-intelligence system. The blackboard does not yet expose a shared spatial occupancy/threat model; organized defense has no dedicated reason or episode evidence. Rest-defense selection is positional rather than a full one-to-one marking allocation. Tactical changes, contested balls and interruptions need broader end-to-end coverage for this new path. Individual perception and communication latency have not been modeled for these duties. Attack candidate generation still uses transient collections, and coordinated attack does not cover the whole team. Paired causal corpus, representative long-horizon performance comparisons, and comprehensive checkpoint/capture/replay validation of the new path remain outstanding. Existing transition checkpoint tests do not alone prove organized-defense restoration.
