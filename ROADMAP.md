# Roadmap

Where FootballEngine is going. The first public demo is planned for January 2027. That date is a goal, not a deadline: the demo ships when it feels finished.

## The demo has to make you feel five things

* **Rivals adapt.** "The other manager read me and I had to change."
* **Matches turn on key moments.** "I made a change at the right moment and turned it around."
* **A living world.** "There is always something to discover."
* **The mod editor.** "I can build my own league."
* **Your project.** "My club finally plays the way I want."

## Done

* Deterministic match engine with 22 thinking players, real ball physics and held-out calibration.
* 3D match viewer with five cameras.
* Career loop: calendar, squad, tactics, training, scouting, transfers, finances, board and news.
* Rival managers with personalities who study you between matches.
* Key-moment pacing: the match stops for chances and manager signals instead of the clock.
* Mod editor for countries, leagues, clubs, kits and squads, with publishing and a local library.

## Next

* **Rivals that adapt during the match,** not only before it.
* **An open-world feel.** People with history, places with character, situations that ask for a decision, a manager who grows, secrets to find.
* **People who remember.** Morale and promises as real state, with consequences you can trace.
* **Original 3D assets.** Replace the generated players, animations, stadium and crowd with our own work.
* **Polish.** Every page reviewed for clarity, one way to do each thing, and English and Spanish everywhere.

## Later

* National teams.
* Full youth system.
* Press conferences.
* Online play, once single player is solid.

## How progress is measured

Each item closes with a test or a measured result, never by eye. Long saves of ten or more seasons run as tests, which the deterministic engine makes cheap.
