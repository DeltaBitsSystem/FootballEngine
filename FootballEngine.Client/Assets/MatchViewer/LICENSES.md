# Match Viewer Asset Licenses

The code-generated pitch and ball, and the player sprite generator, are implemented in this repository. Their entries in `manifest.json` identify their source and the project's internal license reference; the repository currently has no standalone root `LICENSE` file, so distribution rights still need a project-level legal confirmation.

The match stadium is generated at runtime for each home club from the original stadium kit below
(`Match3D/StadiumLayout.fs`, `Match3D/StadiumGeometry.fs`). The earlier unattested `stadium.glb` has been removed from
the product.

No external asset should be added without its exact source, SPDX identifier or full license text, attribution/redistribution terms, source and processed-file hashes, and a clean offline build path. Do not infer permission from the fact that a file is present in the repository.

## Anatomical player pack

`player.glb` and its texture atlases contain adaptations of MakeHuman's CC0 anatomical assets, with FootballEngine-authored equipment, rig adaptation and motion. Exact upstream revision, URLs, SHA-256 checksums and the vendored CC0 legal text are in `Sources/MakeHuman/README.md`, `sources.json` and `LICENSE.ASSETS.md`. No MakeHuman application code or third-party motion is included. `player-motion.json` pins the generated model and texture hashes; the authoring sources are shipped alongside them.

## Surface pack

The pitch turf and concrete layers are original FootballEngine content. The turf is thousands of modelled grass
blades instanced by Geometry Nodes and rendered top-down (albedo, normals, occlusion) by
`Tools/MatchViewer3D/Surfaces/author_turf.py`, converted by `turf_pack.py`; the concrete is the stadium kit's baked
procedural material. `Tools/MatchViewer3D/build_surface_pack.py` builds the GPU mip chains offline from those sources
only; nothing is downloaded. The earlier ambientCG and Poly Haven CC0 sources were removed from the pack.

## Stadium kit, crowd, ball, hairstyles, shirt typeface and dusk/night skies

Original FootballEngine content generated by the scripts under `Tools/MatchViewer3D/` (StadiumKit, Crowd, Ball,
ProAthlete/build_hair_styles.py, Kits, OriginalSky/author_sky_variants.py). Every mesh, rig, animation, texture pixel
and glyph is authored by those scripts from Blender procedural tools; their JSON manifests record hashes and
provenance. The hairstyles are grown from the player pack's own scalp area (itself a documented CC0 MakeHuman
derivative) and share its rig.
